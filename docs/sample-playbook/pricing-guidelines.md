# Pricing Guidelines

**Document type:** Pricing
**Owner:** Revenue Operations
**Last reviewed:** 2026-01-20

## List pricing structure

QuoteIQ's own commercial product (referenced here as the example pricing
model reps should mirror when building client-facing quotations for
professional services) uses three tiers:

- **Starter** — for teams under 10 sales reps, $40/rep/month billed
  annually, or $50/rep/month billed monthly.
- **Growth** — for teams of 10–50 reps, $65/rep/month billed annually.
  Includes the manager dashboard and win/loss analytics.
- **Enterprise** — for teams over 50 reps, custom pricing. Always route
  Enterprise pricing requests to Revenue Operations rather than
  estimating from the Growth tier — Enterprise includes custom SLAs that
  affect price.

## Line-item pricing for services quotations

When quoting professional services (implementation, onboarding,
custom integration work) rather than seat licenses, use day-rate
pricing:

| Role | Standard day rate | Rush day rate (< 2 week turnaround) |
|---|---|---|
| Implementation consultant | $950/day | $1,300/day |
| Solutions architect | $1,400/day | $1,850/day |
| Integration engineer | $1,100/day | $1,500/day |

Rush pricing applies only when the client is requesting a turnaround
faster than our standard 2-week scheduling window, not simply when a
deal is closing quickly. Do not apply rush pricing as a negotiating
lever — it should reflect actual resourcing cost.

## Bundling services with license fees

When a quotation includes both license fees (seats) and implementation
services, apply any negotiated discount to the license line items only.
Services day rates are not typically discounted, since they reflect
actual staff time rather than software margin. If a client is pushing
for a services discount, that should be flagged to a sales manager
rather than applied unilaterally, since it affects staffing margin
directly.

## Minimum deal size

Quotations under $5,000 total contract value should generally not
receive a discount beyond the standard 10% rep-level tier, since the
fixed cost of onboarding a new account doesn't scale down with deal
size. If a very small deal seems to warrant an exception (e.g., a
strategic pilot with a larger prospective account), note the strategic
rationale in the quotation notes.

## Currency and rounding

All list prices above are in USD. When preparing a quotation, round
final line totals to the nearest whole dollar — do not quote clients
fractional cents. Tax should be calculated on the post-discount
subtotal, not the pre-discount list price.
