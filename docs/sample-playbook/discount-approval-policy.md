# Discount Approval Policy

**Document type:** Policy
**Owner:** Revenue Operations
**Last reviewed:** 2026-02-10

## Purpose

This policy defines who can approve discounts on quotations, and at what
thresholds. It applies to all quotations created through QuoteIQ regardless
of client segment.

## Standard discount tiers

| Discount off list price | Approval required |
|---|---|
| 0–10% | Sales rep may apply without approval |
| 11–20% | Sales manager approval required (via quotation status flow) |
| 21–35% | Regional director approval required, logged in deal notes |
| 36%+ | VP Sales approval required; must include a written business justification in the quotation notes field |

Discounts should be entered as a single blended rate on the quotation. Do not
stack a line-item discount and a header-level discount on the same
quotation — this has caused invoicing mismatches in the past and is no
longer supported.

## Segment-specific rules

### Healthcare

Healthcare clients (hospitals, clinics, health networks) are subject to
tighter margin protection because of the extended sales cycles typical in
this segment. For healthcare deals over $50,000 total contract value, the
maximum discount a sales rep can offer without director approval is 15%,
not the standard 20%. This is because healthcare deals in this size band
have historically taken 30% longer to close and carry higher servicing
costs during onboarding.

Healthcare deals under $50,000 follow the standard tiers above.

### Public sector

Public sector and government clients often require most-favored-customer
pricing clauses. Any discount offered to a public sector client must be
matched or exceeded for any other public sector client in the same
fiscal year, so check with Revenue Operations before committing to a
public sector discount above 10%.

### Startups / SMB (under 50 employees)

Reps may offer up to 25% without manager approval for verified startups
under 50 employees, recognizing lower servicing costs and the strategic
value of landing high-growth logos early. Verification means confirming
company size via LinkedIn or the client's own website — self-reported
employee counts from the client are not sufficient.

## Multi-year and renewal discounts

Multi-year commitments (2+ years prepaid or invoiced annually with a
signed multi-year contract) may receive an additional 5% on top of the
tier the deal would otherwise qualify for. This stacks with segment
rules above but not with SMB startup pricing.

Renewals at flat or increased price do not require any approval
regardless of the renewal's discount history. Renewals proposing a
discount deeper than the original contract's discount require the same
approval tier as a new deal at that discount level.

## Escalation path

If a rep believes a deal requires an exception to this policy (e.g., a
strategic logo, a competitive displacement situation, or unusual
contract terms), the rep should note the justification in the
quotation's notes field and tag their sales manager for review before
sending the quotation to the client. Do not send a quotation with a
non-standard discount to a client before internal approval is recorded.
